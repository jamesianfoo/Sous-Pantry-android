## Full description (4000 max)
Sous Pantry is your kitchen's second brain. Scan a receipt or sync your supermarket eReceipt and every item lands in your pantry automatically. Then ask Sous what to cook, and get meals built from what is already on your shelves.

WHAT YOU GET
• Receipt scan and eReceipt sync for Woolworths, Coles, Tesco, Walmart, FairPrice and more
• Barcode lookup for quick single items
• Expiry reminders and a daily pantry audit so nothing gets thrown away
• Ask Sous Anything: meal ideas from your pantry, or paste any recipe link
• What to buy: the exact missing ingredients, sent straight to your shopping list
• Meal planner and cooking streak with awards
• Price trends: see what you spend and where
